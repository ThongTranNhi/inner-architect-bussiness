# INNER ARCHITECT image placement plan

## Asset policy

Use real, brand-owned photography first. Assets marked as placeholders must not be described as photographs of the real INNER ARCHITECT or The Hermit location until the owner confirms that they are genuine. Images containing large amounts of embedded text should not be used as page heroes because they cannot adapt to mobile crops and conflict with the website typography.

## Homepage

### Preferred placements

- Hero: one verified photograph of the real The Hermit interior or a quiet architectural detail, full-bleed, with enough calm negative space for one headline and one action.
- “Người xây dựng hệ thống”: a verified portrait of Võ Khiêm, 4:5 crop, beside the short founder introduction.
- “Hệ thống bốn tầng”: use one architectural image of a doorway, room division or pathway. Do not assign one decorative image to every tier.
- “Phiên tiếp cận”: use a verified consultation room or Tarot table image in a wide 16:9 crop.
- “Tiếp tục khám phá”: use an uneven mosaic combining The Hermit, Journal Tarot photography and one LARA product photograph.

- One environmental portrait at The Hermit, 3:2.
- One working photograph with Tarot cards, 4:5.
- One close detail of hands, notes or teaching materials, 3:2.

Avoid using the supplied green and purple promotional layouts as the main profile images. If the underlying portrait cannot be obtained separately, use the full graphic only inside an archive or press section.

## Excluded from website presentation

- Course slides with purple/black backgrounds and embedded curricula.
- Generic images of group circles, consultations or therapy-like settings when they are not documented INNER ARCHITECT events.
- Abstract “energy” diagrams presented as factual explanations.
- Images whose origin, consent or authenticity cannot be confirmed.

## Crop and layout rules

- Use two dominant ratios across the website: 4:5 for portraits and 16:9 or 3:2 for spaces.
- Keep image corners square.
- Full-bleed images appear at most once every three or four screens.
- Body text stays beside or under images, never over busy photographs.
- On mobile, recrop architectural images vertically rather than shrinking the full desktop crop.
- Provide descriptive alt text based on visible content, not marketing claims.
