# INNER ARCHITECT source synthesis

## Editorial objective

The website first serves people looking for support for themselves. It should help them understand what INNER ARCHITECT is, recognise whether the approach may fit, understand its boundaries and book an initial contact session without pressure.

Founder credentials, training programmes, The Hermit and LARA support trust and exploration. They do not replace the homepage's central visitor journey.

## Content kept and strengthened

- “Không tìm bình an. Xây trật tự bên trong.” remains the central manifesto.
- The homepage retains its own persuasion journey rather than becoming a summary index.
- The four layers remain functional scopes rather than a fixed treatment protocol.
- Tarot remains a reflective tool, not a promise of prediction.
- The initial contact session remains the main conversion.
- Founder content is added where it provides proof and context.
- LARA is positioned as a sensory and creative product line, not a healing requirement.

## Content rewritten for safety and clarity

- “Healing system” language is translated into support, reflection, self-regulation and personal development language where possible.
- Claims involving depression, nervous-system treatment, trauma completion, energy clearing or guaranteed change are not published as clinical facts.
- Spiritual language is framed as symbolism, belief or subjective experience.
- Programme descriptions state their educational and experiential scope.
- Product fragrance descriptions remain sensory rather than therapeutic.
